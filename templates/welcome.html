Transparent Supply Chain

The Flower Collection Process

Every flower submission follows a strict, verifiable lifecycle to ensure zero waste and complete accountability.

1

Temple Submits Flowers

Temple owners weigh and record floral offerings (e.g. Marigolds, Roses, Lotuses) through their private dashboard. The record is instantly broadcast to collectors as Submitted.

2

SONPK Collection & Verification

Designated SONPK field collectors visit the temple premises, inspect the flower batch, record the verified weight and notes, and update the status to Collected.

3

Processing & Administrative Audit

Collected flowers are routed to local bio-processing centers for organic conversion. The Admin Department monitors the entire flow with real-time audit logs and zero duplicate entries.
